# Mybizz FDA — Feature List

Everything a Mybizz FDA client gets for £45 a month, with every team member included. Feature numbers match `mb-1-fda-concept.md`.

---

## Your front desk

- **F-10 Every enquiry handled:** enquiries from every channel arrive in one place, are sorted, and get a reply drafted in your voice, in your customer's own language.
- **F-11 You stay in control:** nothing goes out without your team's approval until you choose otherwise. One click approves a reply. Every approval shows who gave it.
- **F-11 Action queue:** your home screen shows the things that need you today.
- **F-14 Polite follow-up:** when a customer goes quiet, a short, friendly follow-up goes out after the delay you set, and stops the moment the customer replies.

## Your channels

- **F-04 Email:** forward your business email; replies go from your own address.
- **F-05 Website enquiry form:** paste one line of code into your website.
- **F-06 Website chat:** a chat bubble on every page of your website.
- **F-07 Voice chat:** customers can speak to your website chat and hear the answer.
- **F-08 WhatsApp:** your WhatsApp Business number, on Meta's official service.
- **F-09 SMS:** your own phone and number, through the SMSGate app.

## Your customers and bookings

- **F-12 Contacts and leads:** every customer, with a complete history of every message on every channel, notes, tags and a simple pipeline.
- **F-13 Booking:** real free times from each team member's Google Calendar, booked automatically, with confirmations and reminders.
- **F-03 Your knowledge:** your services, FAQs and saved replies. When a new question is answered, save the answer with one click.

## Your numbers

- **F-16 Dashboard:** how fast enquiries are answered, meetings booked, results by channel and activity by team member.

## Your data and your team

- **F-01 Your team:** every team member included, each with their own role and two-step sign-in.
- **F-17 Your data, your storage:** nightly backups to your own Cloudflare storage. Export everything at any time. Leave whenever you like and keep your data.
- **F-15 Your AI, your budget:** use your own OpenRouter key with a monthly spending ceiling.
- **F-19 Languages:** replies in your customer's language; screens in English, with French, German, Spanish and Italian to follow.

## Easy to use

- **F-02 Setup in under 30 minutes:** a guided setup wizard.
- **F-18 Help on every screen:** tooltips and short help videos.
- **Palettes:** choose your colours from eight palettes, in light or dark mode.

## Always

- One to one only: never bulk messages or marketing blasts.
- Never handles payments or card details.
- STOP means stop, at once, on every channel.
- Your website chat always says it is an AI assistant.
