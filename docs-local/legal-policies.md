## 1. Terms of Service

**1.1 Agreement.** These Terms govern the use of Mybizz FDA ("the Service"), provided by the Operator. By creating an account or subscribing, the client agrees to these Terms, the Acceptable Use Policy and the Data Processing Agreement.

**1.2 The Service.** Mybizz FDA receives a client's enquiries by email, website form, website chat, WhatsApp and SMS; drafts replies with artificial intelligence; keeps contact records; and arranges meetings in connected Google Calendars. Each client receives its own instance of the Service with its own database.

**1.3 Approval and responsibility.** New accounts start in approve-before-send mode. AI-drafted replies are suggestions. The client is responsible for every message sent from its account, whether approved by a team member or sent by an automatic setting the client switched on.

**1.4 Third-party services.** The client brings and pays for its own OpenRouter key for AI replies, its own WhatsApp Business account with Meta, its own Android phone and phone plan for SMS, its own Google Calendar and its own Cloudflare account for storage. The client keeps those accounts active and pays their charges directly.

**1.5 Price and billing.** The Service costs one fixed monthly price per client, shown at checkout in the client's market currency, with every team member included. Paddle.com is the Operator's online reseller and the merchant of record for every order: Paddle handles checkout, sales tax and VAT, and billing enquiries. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled.

**1.6 Non-payment.** If a payment fails, the client is warned at once. If it is still unpaid after 7 days, the Service is suspended and stops sending and receiving. If it is still unpaid after 37 days, the client's instance is removed after a final backup to the client's own storage. The client's data is never withheld.

**1.7 Data ownership.** The client owns all data it puts into the Service. The client may export its data at any time. Backups are written nightly to the client's own Cloudflare storage, which the client keeps when it leaves.

**1.8 Suspension for misuse.** The Operator may suspend an account at once, without refund, for any breach of the Acceptable Use Policy.

**1.9 Availability and liability.** The Service is provided "as is" and "as available". To the extent the law allows, the Operator's total liability for any claim is limited to the fees the client paid in the three months before the claim.

**1.10 Changes.** The Operator gives 30 days' notice by email of any change to these Terms that reduces the client's rights.

**1.11 Governing law.** These Terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales, without affecting mandatory protections in the client's own country.

## 2. Acceptable Use Policy

**2.1 One to one only.** The Service is for replying to people who contacted the client, booking meetings with them and following up with them one to one. The client must never use it for marketing campaigns, cold outreach, bulk or broadcast messages, or messages to bought or scraped lists. The Service enforces this with hourly sending limits and has no list-sending function.

**2.2 Opt-out.** The client must respect every opt-out. The Service stops all automatic messages to a person who replies STOP or an equivalent, and the client must not contact that person through the Service again unless the person asks.

**2.3 Prohibited content.** The client must not use the Service to request, send or store payment card numbers, bank credentials, passwords or special category data; to deceive or defraud; or for anything unlawful.

**2.4 Platform rules.** The client must follow Meta's WhatsApp Business policies, its phone network's rules and applicable law on electronic marketing and data protection, including UK and EU GDPR and the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

**2.5 Reporting abuse.** Report misuse to `abuse@mybizz.live`.

## 3. Privacy Policy

**3.1 Who is responsible.**
- For the client's own account, sign-in, billing and support details, the Operator is the controller.
- For the client's customers' data inside Mybizz FDA (contacts, messages, bookings), the client is the controller and the Operator is the processor, acting only on the client's instructions under the Data Processing Agreement.

**3.2 What the Operator holds about clients.** Business name, contact name, email address, phone number, billing address, Paddle customer and subscription references, subscription status, instance address, usage and health figures, and support messages. Paddle holds all payment details.

**3.3 What the Service processes for clients.** Customers' names, contact details, messages, booking details and notes, inside the client's own instance, and the client's encrypted keys for its connected services.

**3.4 Purposes.** To provide, secure and support the Service; to bill through Paddle; to send service and security notices.

**3.5 AI.** Customer messages are sent to the AI provider the client chose (through the client's own OpenRouter key) and, for voice, to Groq, only to draft or transcribe a reply. Prompts and AI replies are not logged. No client or customer data is used to train AI models by the Operator.

**3.6 Where data is held.** Client instances run on Hetzner servers in the EU. Backups are held in the client's own Cloudflare account. Sub-processors are listed in section 5.

**3.7 Retention.** Client account records are kept while the subscription is active and for six years after it ends for tax records. Customer data stays in the client's instance while the subscription is active and is deleted when the instance is removed; the client's own backups remain under the client's control.

**3.8 Rights.** People have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or erase their data, and to object to its use. Clients contact `support@mybizz.live`. A client's customer contacts the client, as controller; the Operator helps the client respond.

**3.9 Complaints.** A complaint may be made to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or to the data protection authority of the person's own country.

## 4. Data Processing Agreement

**4.1 Roles.** The client is the controller and the Operator is the processor of the personal data the client's instance processes.

**4.2 Instructions.** The Operator processes that data only to provide the Service and on the client's documented instructions, which are these Terms and the client's settings.

**4.3 Confidentiality.** Only the Operator and its sub-processors have access, each bound to confidentiality.

**4.4 Security.** Each client has its own instance and database; data is encrypted in transit and at rest; keys are held encrypted; sign-in requires two factors; every action is recorded with the person or system that took it.

**4.5 Sub-processors.** The Operator uses the sub-processors in section 5 and gives clients 30 days' notice of any addition, during which the client may object and cancel.

**4.6 Assistance.** The Operator helps the client answer people's requests to exercise their rights and meet its security, breach-notification and impact-assessment duties.

**4.7 Breaches.** The Operator tells the client without undue delay, and within 48 hours, of a personal data breach affecting the client's data.

**4.8 Transfers.** Where a sub-processor processes data outside the UK or EEA, the transfer relies on adequacy decisions or the standard contractual clauses with the UK addendum.

**4.9 End of service.** On cancellation, the client's instance writes a final backup to the client's own storage, and the Operator then deletes the instance and its database.

**4.10 Audit.** The Operator provides the information needed to show compliance with this agreement on reasonable request.

## 6. Cancellation and Refund Policy

**6.1 Cancellation.** The client may cancel at any time in Settings or through the Paddle receipt link. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month; the client is not charged again.

**6.2 Refunds.** Monthly fees are not refunded once a month has started, except where the law requires a refund or where a fault in the Service stopped it working as described and the Operator could not fix the fault within 14 days.

**6.3 How refunds are paid.** Paddle pays approved refunds to the original payment method. Requests go to `support@mybizz.live` with the order number.
