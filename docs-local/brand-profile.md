---

## 2. The Problem Mybizz FDA Solves

- Enquiries arrive on several channels and wait hours for a reply, while the customer contacts a competitor.
- The person who should answer is busy doing the work.
- Booking a first call takes several messages.
- Customers who go quiet are never followed up.

---

## 3. Who It Is For

- Independent consultants and coaches.
- Small agencies: marketing, design, development, recruitment, advisory, accounting and bookkeeping.
- Fractional executives, advisers and professional-service firms.
- Service businesses that book appointments.
- One person to a team of about ten.

---

## 4. Brand Story

Mybizz was built from lived operational experience. Before building software, the founder spent years setting up and running life-support operations at remote sites, from the Congo jungle to Antarctica. There, systems had to be simple, self-contained and dependable, because a missed message meant failure.

Small service businesses lose work for the same reason: messages missed while the owner is busy. Mybizz FDA is the front desk that never misses one.

---

## 5. Personality and Tone of Voice

- **Character:** clear, calm, practical, honest and human: a dependable front-desk partner.
- **Say:** missed enquiries, faster replies, booked calls, you approve, your data.
- **Avoid:** technical jargon ("LLM", "agents", "orchestration", "OpenRouter" in headlines), hype, exaggerated promises, pushy selling.
- **Promises the brand keeps:** one to one only; nothing sent without approval until the client chooses; STOP means stop; the client owns its data; no payments handled.

---

## 6. Messages

- **Primary:** Never miss a qualified enquiry again.
- **Control:** Your AI front desk drafts the reply; you approve it with one click.
- **Speed:** Answer in minutes, not hours, on every channel.
- **Booking:** Real free times from your calendar, booked without the back-and-forth.
- **Ownership:** Your data stays yours, backed up to your own storage every night.
- **Price:** £45 a month, your whole team included.

---

## 7. Visual Identity

- **Design system:** Material Design 3 through `m3-custom-theme`.
- **Default palette:** Professional Blue, on mybizz.live and in every Mybizz FDA screen. Clients may choose any of the eight theme palettes: Default, Professional Blue, Warm Coral, Calm Teal, Sophisticated Purple, Sky Blue, Forest Green and Desert Rose, each in light and dark.
- **Mode:** light or dark follows the device.
- **Typography:** Roboto throughout.
- **Imagery:** real working people and plain screenshots of the product; no stock robots or glowing AI imagery.

---

## 8. Ownership and Trust Promises

- Each client has its own copy of Mybizz FDA with its own database; no data is shared between clients.
- Hosted in the EU.
- Nightly backups go to the client's own Cloudflare storage; the client keeps them when it leaves.
- Month to month, no contract.
- Paddle handles checkout, tax and invoices.
